Policies

Booking, payment and cancellation

Plain-language rules so there are no surprises.

How booking works

  1. You send a booking request with your dates and details.
  2. We check availability and reply .
  3. If your room is available, we send you a secure payment link.
  4. You pay by card, bank or bank transfer. Once we verify the payment, your booking is confirmed.

A request is not a confirmed booking. A room is only reserved for you once payment has been received and verified.

Prices

Prices are shown per room, per night, in Nigerian Naira (₦). The total you see on the booking page is the amount we will ask you to pay. We will tell you before you pay if anything changes.

Payment

After we confirm availability, we hold your room for hours while you pay. Card, bank and USSD payments are handled by Paystack on their secure window. We never see or store your card details. If you prefer, you can pay by bank transfer and send us your proof of payment with your booking reference.

Check-in and check-out

Cancellation and changes

Sample policy: you may change or cancel for free up to 48 hours before check-in. After that, the first night may be non-refundable. Please contact us as early as possible if your plans change and quote your booking reference.

Identification

Guests may be asked to show a valid ID at check-in.

Privacy

This website does not store your booking details on a server. When you send a request, the details you enter are placed in a WhatsApp (or email) message to us, so we can respond to you. Payment details are handled by Paystack. We use your details only to manage your booking and to contact you about it.
