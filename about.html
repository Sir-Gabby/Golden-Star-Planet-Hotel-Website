About us

A place to rest, meet and feel looked after

Golden Star Planet Hotel and Suite is built around one idea: a stay should be simple, comfortable and fair from the moment you book.

Our story

Whether you're travelling for business, celebrating a special occasion or simply need a quiet place to stay, we want you to feel welcome. Our rooms and suites are prepared with care, and our team is on hand to help with whatever you need.

We keep booking honest and easy. You see the price before you ask, we confirm availability before you pay, and you pay in the way that suits you best: card, bank or transfer.

What we promise

  • Clear prices. What you see is what you pay per night.
  • Pay after confirmation. No payment until your room is confirmed.
  • Clean, comfortable rooms. Prepared properly for every guest.
  • Helpful people. Real answers, quickly, on WhatsApp or by phone.
